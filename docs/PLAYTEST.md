# Playtest checklist (about 20 minutes)

For Nathan and his son (ROADMAP step 1). Play, then tell Claude or Chet what felt wrong, what felt great, and anything confusing. Short notes are fine ("the fly flew off every time", "slow motion is awesome"). This also covers the checks only a person can do: U01, W01, E02 and M04 in `docs/SPEC.md` section 25.

Before starting: double-click `update.bat`, then `play.bat`.

## 1. A normal hunt (5 min)

- [ ] Walk along the sill, then creep with Shift toward a fly. Freeze when it looks your way.
- [ ] Hold Space to aim at the fly, then let go to jump. Did you catch it?
- [ ] If it struggles, tap Space to keep your grip. Did it feel fair?
- [ ] Press E to eat.
- [ ] *Note:* why did a hunt work, or fail? (E02)

## 2. Through the spider's eyes (5 min)

- [ ] Press **V** for first person, and V again for third person. Is the line in the top corner easy to see?
- [ ] Look around with the mouse. Only two thin strips are sharp: that's where the front eyes point.
- [ ] Look far to one side and stand still: does the spider turn to face it?
- [ ] Hunt a fly in first person.
- [ ] Climb a wall in first person. Is the turning comfortable, or dizzying?

## 3. Slow motion (3 min)

- [ ] Press **T** during a hunt. Everything slows: the spider, the flies, the jump. Press T again for normal speed.
- [ ] Try a jump at a fly in slow motion.
- [ ] Esc → Slow-motion speed: try 25 % and 75 %.

## 4. Controller only, sound off (5 min, if a controller is around) — U01

- [ ] Put the keyboard and mouse aside. Walk, aim and jump (A), eat (X), switch view (Y) and use slow motion (right trigger).
- [ ] In the Esc menu, change one button under Keys and buttons…, and use it.
- [ ] Turn "Jump aim toggles" on in the Esc menu and do a hunt with it.
- [ ] *Note:* was anything impossible without the keyboard?
- [ ] *Note:* with the computer muted, did anything get missed? (The game has no sound yet, so this should be fine.)

## 5. Home, and the shadow (2 min) — W01

- [ ] Walk into the silk-lined crack at the west end and press E to rest. It says "Checkpoint kept".
- [ ] Wander off, then Esc → Return to checkpoint. Are you back where you rested?
- [ ] After about a minute a shadow crosses the sill. Did you notice the warning before it came?

## 6. The camera (1 min) — M04

- [ ] In third person, walk under the leaf and against walls. Does the camera ever end up inside something, or lose the spider?

## Afterwards

- The best moment.
- The most annoying moment.
- What you would change first.
- For the eye view: keep it, change it, or both?
