# Typed archive collection and publication

This extends [the entry/lease candidate](ENTRY-WORKERS.md) with actual replacements for the source collector, archive repository and archive controller. The complete gate still is **not installed in ordinary Runtime, server account lanes or engine startup**. Commit, Pipeline, ordinary/current/progress saving, New and departure remain unconverted. Nothing here proves online durability, native loading speed or a published release.

## Nested starts and state

`operation.luau` is a retained-operation bridge with bounded, plain counter descriptors. It always charges the session's global counter and may charge up to seven additional parent/child counters at the same provider boundary. Duplicate descriptors, marker/counter aliases, malformed values and exhausted caps refuse. Descriptor metadata and backend methods are captured. Session/account/epoch/backend/scheduler/counter identity and the original job guard are checked again after scheduler bookkeeping. Provider failures remain backend failures, with existing retry/backoff semantics. Capacity waits have no provider attempt, failure or counter effect.

An explicit method binding can supply a raw-write adapter without changing `session.backend`. This chooses an operation's backend function; it does not grant authority or replace selected-key, lease, source, transform or readback checks. Updates still consume both typed units and recheck the job before/after every callback. Counter rows and an optional dispatch marker are written together, with no external callback between them and the captured backend method.

`collector.luau` keeps every original collect/verify read and the resumable bounds/copy/comparison work. Its `reads`, the parent controller's `requests` and the session counter advance once at a real start. Retrying a failed read does not confuse unavailable capacity with another failure. Changing an unselected source refuses before publication.

`repository.luau` stays `pending` while its write is queued. It keeps the same owned item, callback and outcome record across polls. The dispatch marker changes it to `running` only at a real provider start, blocking a reentrant step. Insert-if-absent/conflict checks, fresh readback after success or ambiguous acknowledgement, full equality checks and complete-index verification remain unchanged. No update result alone verifies an archive part. Cancellation after a committed part retains that part and debt, drains the provider and never completes the index or reports success.

`controller.luau` supplies shared parent accounting to both children and separates ownership-renewal and ordinary-read operation handles. Collection cannot begin before the first acknowledged ownership check. Afterwards, an unavailable write allowance can delay renewal while CPU work and independently eligible reads continue under the existing unexpired fence. The retained renewal callback timestamps its candidate when it actually executes, rather than using a stale queued time. Total request/deadline bounds, initial/full-original matching and read-only complete-original reuse remain enforced. Parent/global/provider counts are checked after every test step, including child failures.

## Bounded scheduler history

Typed paths no longer call the old aggregate `budgetOk` to decide admission. That exposed a memory issue: the original scheduler's `begin` only appends, and its rolling cleanup used to occur inside `budgetOk`. `worker-v3.luau` and the nested bridge now validate and prune the plain timestamp history before and after bookkeeping, without invoking the old admission callback or changing the typed gate's debt. Lifetime counters remain monotonic; only timestamps at least sixty seconds old expire. A malformed, metatable-backed, sparse, nonfinite or oversized history fails closed before storage and is not erased. The scan is bounded, and a wrapper cannot keep appending indefinitely after the bound is exceeded.

`history-before-fix-*` retains a failing run against the preceding worker and nested bridge. Its exact original tests are preserved in `history-before-tests.luau`, with their hash matching the original receipt. The passing counterpart exercises180 reads in each bridge: the scheduler retains the last60 timestamps, lifetime counts stay180, and a replacement scope retains typed rolling debt. Additional malformed-history checks are included. Use `worker-v3.luau` for new integrations; earlier worker versions remain solely to bind their historical evidence.
