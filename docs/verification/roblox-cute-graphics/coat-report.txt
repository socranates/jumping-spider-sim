tools/preview/coat.py: how the abdomen's coat treats the white pattern, the black body and the outline.
Godot-shim renders of the part list, not Roblox rendering; they compare two coats and do not say how fuzzy either looks.
Per layer: hairs only, fur shells only, both. White: share of the designed white still as bright as designed (85 % or more) and its mean brightness.
Shards: pixels the coat made 40/255 or more brighter than the bare body, away from the white. Black: mean lift of the black body.
Outline: reach past the bare silhouette at a faint (12/255) and a solid (60/255) visibility.
In the top view the white spots are as white as the background, so the silhouette comes from the bare body alone.
