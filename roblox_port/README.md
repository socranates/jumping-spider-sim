# The Windowsill — active Roblox project

Open **TheWindowsill.rbxlx** in Roblox Studio and press **Play**. After updating Git, close/reopen the place so Studio reads the new file. Keep this folder name: `.gdignore` keeps the frozen Godot project from importing it. Scale remains 1 Godot WU = 1 stud; gravity is 35.

## Controls: one scheme, as in the Godot game

Every action of the frozen Godot game (`scripts/core/input_setup.gd` and its README) against the port. Keyboard and mouse, a controller and touch work together: an action is held while any of them holds it (ROBLOX-08). The words on screen name the device you used last. H shows or hides the controls card.

## The look: a cute, fuzzy spider and a lit windowsill (ROBLOX-13, ROBLOX-14)

The spider keeps the daughter's design exactly (tests/look.luau pins it) and is cuter and fuzzier: a bigger, rounder head, big glossy eyes with catchlights, fluffy palps, a bold jumper's tufts behind the eyes (no eyebrows, mouth or smile: docs/SPEC.md), a short coat of thin flat locks combed toward the rear (black on the black areas, white only on the white spots and the band), a few guard hairs, a short side fringe and four faint dark fur shells that soften the outline, sized for the 5-stud camera, hairy legs, and a little idle motion (breathing, a slow head tilt, palp drumming) when it stands still. The idle motion is drawn-rig only; feet, root part and movement never see it. Every number is a knob in `src/Shared/Data/Appearances.luau`.

The world is drawn from `src/Shared/Data/Graphics.luau` (the sill's paint, the window, the view, the lighting), `Data/Flora.luau` (the Pilea, the trough's planting, the garden outside the window) and `Data/Wear.luau` (wear, condensation, silk, coin, fallen leaves) with Roblox's own features only: a material per surface (PBR through `MaterialService.Use2022Materials`, set in both Rojo projects), Future lighting (set in the Rojo projects), a soft warm sun picked to shine in through the window (`Habitat/SunPick`; the choice is in Lighting's attribute `SunPick` and Output), Atmosphere haze, Sky, clouds, bloom, colour grade and sun rays; a drawn window wall with glass and a view (sky, hills, houses, trees) while the physical back wall stays solid, climbable and invisible; dust motes and light shafts. The look is a worn, sunlit windowsill at the spider's scale (ROBLOX-21): cream enamel with bare wood showing at chips and along the front lip, grime in the corners and dust bunnies; the three Pilea leaves drawn as glossy pancakes with veins, a darker rim, a lighter underside and petioles over their pieces (`Habitat/Plants`; the pieces turn invisible, still solid and climbable, only when the drawing was built); a terracotta trough with a damp stain, soil, pebbles, moss and seedlings; outside the window a weathered window box of nine black-eyed Susans, with an invisible Neon ultraviolet layer (`Workspace.Decor.UVLayer`, attribute `UVOpacity`, for the spider-eye view), and flowering shrubs; dark wood and a silk retreat in the crevice; a copper coin, fallen leaves, paint chips, a crack and crumbs; condensation and smudges on the glass (`Habitat/Patina`). All decor is non-colliding, untouchable and invisible to rays. The macro depth of field is on in the full tier unless the Workspace attribute `DepthOfField` is false. The looks are best effort: a lighting or decor failure is warned about in Output and the old look stays (the solid back wall stays visible if the drawn wall fails).

The flies are house flies (ROBLOX-22), 0.7 cm long, drawn by `src/Client/FlyBodies.luau` from the numbers in `src/Shared/Data/FlyLook.luau`: ellipsoids and rods, 39 parts a fly (four flies, 156 parts), the same at both detail tiers. A head with two big glossy red-brown compound eyes and a pale silver face stripe, a grey thorax with four dark stripes (thin slices that follow its curve), a grey-brown abdomen with a darker stripe down its back, a short dark proboscis, eight tiny bristles, six two-segment legs standing on the surface, and two thin glass wings (transparency 0.55, a dark vein along the front edge, a faint iridescent film after Shevtsova et al. 2011, PNAS 108:668-673) folded back in a shallow V. In the air each wing is a soft translucent blur that trembles a little (a beating wing would only strobe at 60 frames a second), the legs tuck in, the fly keeps its back to the sky (the normal it carries is that of the surface it last stood on) and the soft dark oval that stands in for the shadow on the surface (the parts cast none) goes. Every part is anchored, non-colliding, untouchable, invisible to rays and casts no shadow; a fly's position, heading and normal still come from the prey simulation (the prey profile stays species-neutral: this is only how the prototype is drawn) and nothing here feeds back. `tests/flies.luau` pins it.

Detail tiers: touch-only devices (phones, tablets) get the low tier, anything else the full tier; the Workspace attribute `Detail` set to "low" or "full" overrides the guess. Full is 624 spider parts (475 hairs) and 806 decor parts (budget 1,500); low is 266 spider parts (145 hairs, no standing guard hairs, one fur shell, no idle motion) and 151 decor parts (budget 450), without bloom, sun rays, clouds, shafts, depth of field, the small details, the wear, the glass condensation, the leaves' veins and the finer planting. Nothing has been seen in Roblox Studio or timed on a device.

The first appearance is `src/Shared/Data/Appearances.luau`, separate from species and physics. It keeps the selected black-and-white pattern at every recorded stage. The rig uses anchored noncolliding parts and one BulkMoveTo per frame. Growth, hunger/softness and full pose/twitch parity still need the LIFE port.

## The day, rest and checkpoints (ROBLOX-24, ROBLOX-25; 2026-10-16)

The windowsill has a day: it starts at 9:00 on day 1 and about 12 waking hours pass in 15 minutes of play (the day is in the top-left corner). Hunger runs by the hour (the reserve can run out; nothing follows yet). In the silk-lined crack at the west end, standing still with nothing in the jaws, E rests; in the evening and at night it sleeps until morning, and the night passes in about 8 seconds. The light follows the hour (`Data/Graphics.dayNight`, first guesses until seen in Studio) and the spider's eyes reach a third as far at night. Starting a rest and each morning keep a checkpoint, kept in memory for the session (no DataStore); Backspace returns to it. See `docs/HANDOFF-roblox-life.md`.

- LIFE checks: `python tests/run.py path/to/luau life.luau` prints `LIFE passed=20846 failed=0`: LifeClock, NeedsModel and the rest-and-sleep loop against `tests/life_reference.json` (made by `tools/export_life_reference.gd` from the frozen Godot code: `godot --headless --path . -s roblox_port/tools/export_life_reference.gd -- roblox_port/tests/life_reference.json`, then `python3 tools/reference_to_luau.py tests/life_reference.json`), the day's light, the words and the retreat box.
- Checkpoint checks: `python tests/run.py path/to/luau checkpoint.luau` prints `CHECKPOINT passed=123 failed=0`: a whole world on the box sill kept, run on, put back and run again exactly; refusals; the store; the DataStore stand-in.

## A life and continuing (ROBLOX-33, 34)

The start menu offers Standard and Free play (gentle hunger; the wasp only touches). **M / View / PlayStation Touchpad / Menu** returns to the life menu. Growing to adult in a Standard main life opens Easy for this session; Free play is available immediately. Progress and checkpoints are **session only**, not saved across Roblox sessions.

Home is the silk-framed opening **under** the wooden roof at the west end, not a decorative paint crack or the roof itself. Walk inside, stop with no prey in the jaws, and press **E / L3 / Rest**. C / R1 / Change while resting awake chooses the peacock; X / R1 / Dance outside starts the dance. It is still free/unlocked for testing.

Standard shows a starvation warning, then a full day of weakness before death. The end offers the last rest (when one exists) or an unrelated spider in a new spot. The appearance stays chosen. Main life and Free play have separate last rests; starting a new main life asks for confirmation. The menu has large text, scrolling and controller focus. Notebook and live DataStore persistence remain later work.

## Growth and peacock polish (ROBLOX-35/36)

Actual food energy, accumulated until 0.9, readies the next moult (the amount of energy granted matters, not the meal count). Rest in the safe crack to moult. Twelve game hours pass in about nine real seconds; movement waits until the spider finishes. Each of three moults grows the drawn spider, leaves an old skin and gives ten game hours of pale soft skin with shorter jump reach. Stage scales 1/1.15/1.32/1.50 are artistic, with the same collider and world-planted feet. Actual adulthood opens Easy for a Standard main life. Checkpoint schema 3 keeps this state; older records begin at the youngest stage without crediting old meals again. Everything remains session-only.

The peacock gets a closer camera, richer fan colours and six fine scale highlights at full detail (119 parts; mobile stays 80). Room ambience is quieter; the eight-second wasp warning is unchanged. The stage and notices share one HUD column. The cosmetic peacock's adult male colours/dance remain available at every gameplay stage, deliberately: this is an appearance choice, not a biological Maratus life simulation.
