# All-slot recovery owner

Private implementation on production source `8c9b71896736d85d43e02fec1521bcd567cdb33f`. Nothing here installs recovery in the engine or menu, publishes a place, or changes ordinary controls. The current production candidate and its completed Windows/Linux/Studio evidence remain in `docs/restored-slot-ownership/`.

`recovery-profile-owner-prototype.luau` extends the existing service owner. The server supplies all three trusted destination templates and contexts at construction. Templates are bounded, copied and frozen; clients cannot supply a destination. Discovery shares one SaveSession, tracked backend, scheduler, progress baseline and request quota. It coalesces at most three queued slots and gives an explicit player choice priority. A new pipeline captures the chosen profile; changing only `runtime.recovery.context` would leave the old pipeline's destination in use.

Discovery retains earlier published observations. Refreshing unchanged storage reuses its offer ID. Selecting Continue always initiates a fresh read after that choice. Only the exact displayed ID can proceed; a changed record requires another explicit choice. There is one globally increasing choice serial across slots, while the underlying Session retains its existing per-slot intents. A newer choice revokes older authority and immediately cancels an outstanding reader. Binding waits for the provider and all worker locks to drain. Reentrant notification callbacks also yield to the newest choice.

Before migration accepts any writes, an ordinary empty-slot New Life choice can resume the same normal worker while retaining other protected slots. After acceptance, switching modes requires a future explicit service/client replacement epoch. The owner revokes old credentials, keeps its enforcing hold, and uses the existing lease-only helper to renew P metadata without writing rests, proposals or archives. It can take the existing closed departure route. It does not silently resume an older world or invent a new session.

`recovery-profile-owner-tests.luau` covers all-slot discovery, unchanged IDs, each destination, global ordering, a suspended read, changed storage, preaccept ordinary routing, a reentrant newer choice, Easy eligibility, unnegotiated peers, invalid catalogs, and a 150-second simulated postaccept wait followed by departure. `recovery-profile-service-tests.luau` uses the actual SaveService, SaveClient, DataStoreBackend and raw adapter over memory. For each mode, all three offers coexist in one visit; application happens once; the exact receipt receives authority; the next rest gets a stored acknowledgement; other current slots and immutable originals remain unchanged. These are synthetic-clock, opaque compression fixtures, not native networking or measured online loading times.
