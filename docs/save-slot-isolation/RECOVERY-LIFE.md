# Private life-state projection

The private life preparer now combines the validated recorded-world inspector and portable-meal component into an owned data capsule. It does not build or apply a complete destination world, write a save, grant a life credential or enable Continue. Preparation leaves the current Preserve and the complete original checkpoint untouched.

## Field mapping

`recovery-life-fields.json` enumerates every supported LifeLoop field and its action. Unknown fields refuse. The same rule covers the clock, growth, mating counters and rare-visit record, so new state cannot silently disappear through an older mapper.

The capsule preserves the exact day/hour/sleeping clock state, rest/elapsed/checkpoint counts, instar, credited food, ready flag, remaining moult and soft-cuticle hours, and growth rules/threshold configuration. Run and lock availability remain derived from the retained instar. Reserves, meals and grantedSeen use the separately owned portable-meal graph; the life and feeding objects both point to its single reserve object. No old food is newly credited.

Generation number, born/mealsBase grace, cold-day history, lineage start-zone/region, mating stage and every supported brood/visit/courtship counter remain exact. Rare serial/generation/birth identity and any recorded visit are retained. Origin metadata retains the source world/seed/revision/region/retreat and optional persisted life token. Consumers receive their own origin table. This preserves data; correct future rare scheduling also depends on the global RareLedger and source seed, which the complete destination transaction must bind rather than reroll.

Old exuviae are copied into a historical skins root. The active portable growth object starts with no old-world skin positions. Completing its retained moult adds the next skin at the destination position; it does not move an old skin there. Rival and mate records, including courtship meter and encounter counters, are retained in explicit encounter-history roots. They are absent from the portable active life object. Wasp/cat/water/breakaway checkpoint records are also retained in history when present. These historical records are not permission to spawn actors, replay a mating scene or apply old geometry inside the Preserve.

Preparation rebinds the reserve reference and omits the running program's save callback. It clears UI notices/events, pending checkpoint results, asleep presentation and checkpoint-due flags, derives light from the retained clock and sets normal recovery grace. It preserves resting state, especially while moulting. Pending checkpoint preparation refuses. This matches the distinction between durable life state and transient presentation; callbacks, live actors and scene placement must be supplied by the destination builder.

Recorded inspection happens before projection, using the supported recorded map without switching the globally active map. Origin metadata supports only known worlds/revisions and valid persisted life tokens. The decoder checks capsule/root identities, supported fields, mode/rules, growth, generation grace, mating/rare invariants and the shared reserve identity. It is a private in-process decoder, not a new untrusted transport boundary; aggregate sizes and authoritative archive/source binding remain the integrating transaction's responsibility. Historical actor geometry was validated during recorded inspection and must never be treated as validated against current Preserve geometry.

## Evidence and remaining work

The current CLI bundle passes 360 life checks plus the 49 meal regressions. It includes all six mating stages across Normal, Free play and Easy; unchanged clock/growth/unlocks, generation grace, mating/brood counters, rare identity data, actor/skin history separation and source/map preservation. A moult with six hours left completes after those six hours and creates its next skin at a new position. Tests also cover unknown fields that the general checkpoint inspector accepts, rule disagreement, malformed origin/capsule data, independent consumption, wasp visit/escape history and 130 cooperative suspensions without changing the source or live map.

The first draft tried to freeze the already frozen meal subcapsule again and failed before exposing a result. The corrected recursive freeze preserves ownership and accepts already frozen tables. That original source/failure is retained; `verify-recovery-life.py` reconstructs both executed bundles from current inputs and validates their hashes. Compilation and full strict analysis pass with all four private candidate modules in a disposable source tree. These are CLI fixtures, not Nathan's actual-save migration, native performance evidence or a natural complete lifecycle playthrough.

This is still not complete projection. Next, build fresh authored Preserve ecology, attach this life/reserve data under an explicit encounter policy, preserve notebook/global rare history, bind source seed and selected life identity, and produce a checkpoint accepted by the destination's full validator. Courtship/mating/hatched stages need explicit encounter/ending behavior when attached; retaining their records is not proof those scenes resume correctly. Archive retention and an atomic destination transaction, native transport, isolated real DataStore/rejoin, full release gates, main/publication and laptop synchronization remain open. Production runtime and ordinary cross-world Continue protection are unchanged.
