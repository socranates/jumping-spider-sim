# The Windowsill — active Roblox project

Open **TheWindowsill.rbxlx** in Roblox Studio and press **Play**. After updating Git, close/reopen the place so Studio reads the new file. Keep this folder name: `.gdignore` keeps the frozen Godot project from importing it. Scale remains 1 Godot WU = 1 stud; gravity is 35.

## Where it stands (2026-10-19)

A whole life can be played: the spider and its day, hunger, rest and sleep, three moults, the mud dauber wasp (twice a day), the brown rival, a mate and the mating cycle, the field notebook, Settings, Inspect, ambient music, and saves between visits. It has been published to the private playtest place (v30, the audit round, is the last build on record). Nathan has played the sill, music and detail rounds (ROBLOX-71 to 90) live, but none of them has had a Studio engine-check or device pass, and the audit round (ROBLOX-91 to 93) was published as v30 without one; the world core (ROBLOX-94, the first open-world round) has not been run in Studio, beyond the glass (ROBLOX-95, W2) was built and published as v32 without a Studio run, garden life (ROBLOX-96, W3) was built and published as v33 without a Studio run, and the bestiary's part A (ROBLOX-101, W3b) and the look round (ROBLOX-103, W3c) were built into the places for v34 without a Studio run, and the bestiary's part B (ROBLOX-105) has not been built into the places, published or run in Studio at all; the live place now carries a self-test for Studio (ROBLOX-104) so each publish can be walked in the real engine by pressing Play: the last accepted Studio run on record is the dragline round's (111/111 engine and 29/29 live-client checks, `docs/verification/roblox-dragline/RESULTS.md`). The sections below are dated snapshots: each says what was true when it was written, and a note in brackets says where that has moved; "Since then" at the end gathers the later rounds. Decisions are in `docs/DECISIONS.md` (ROBLOX-xx), the newest handoff is `docs/HANDOFF-roblox-world.md` (the detail round's is `docs/HANDOFF-roblox-detail.md`) and the running log is `docs/SESSION_LOG.md`.

The world (ROBLOX-94, `docs/OPEN_WORLD_DESIGN.md`): the sill is region 0 of one continuous garden. `src/Shared/World/WorldMap.luau` is the authored map (one region so far, `sill`, with the layout's own bounds, spawn, crevice and a clock scale of 1.0), `World/Region.luau` the generators (the sill's return `Habitat/WindowsillLayout`'s lists themselves, byte for byte, plus the rival's keep-out box and the part budgets), `World/WorldBuild.luau` builds every region's boxes under one `Workspace.World` folder on the server (`src/Server/Main.server.luau`; the sill's folder inside it is the same "Windowsill" with the same 28 parts), `src/Server/WorldFocus.luau` parks a replication focus part per player at the sill's spawn for instance streaming (`Workspace.StreamingEnabled`, min radius 96, target 160, authored in both Rojo projects), and `src/Client/WorldClient.luau` is the client's side: the folder every ray includes, the region the spider is in and the pace of its day (`LifeClock.game.clockScale`), the streaming waits and focus. The drawn sill, `Workspace.Decor`, is a persistent Model. A rest records `world_map = { seed, revision, region, retreat, diffs }`; a save from another world revision restores at its region's spawn. ## The look: a cute, fuzzy spider and a lit windowsill (ROBLOX-13, ROBLOX-14)

The spider keeps the daughter's design exactly (tests/look.luau pins it) and is cuter and fuzzier: a bigger, rounder head, big glossy eyes with catchlights, fluffy palps, a bold jumper's tufts behind the eyes (no eyebrows, mouth or smile: docs/SPEC.md), a short coat of thin flat locks combed toward the rear (black on the black areas, white only on the white spots and the band), a few guard hairs, a short side fringe and four faint dark fur shells that soften the outline, sized for the 5-stud camera, hairy legs, and a little idle motion (breathing, a slow head tilt, palp drumming) when it stands still. The idle motion is drawn-rig only; feet, root part and movement never see it. Every number is a knob in `src/Shared/Data/Appearances.luau`. [Since ROBLOX-84 to 87 the first spider and the tarantulas also have knees, tapered legs, feet and fangs, hairs that lie along the body (the tarantulas wear Fabric fur shells and bristles) and living motion in `Rig/Living`: a breathing abdomen, grooming, a turn toward what you look at and raised legs at a wasp's first warning. `tests/look.luau` and `tests/idle.luau` pin them.]

The world is drawn from `src/Shared/Data/Graphics.luau` (the sill's paint, the window, the view, the lighting), `Data/Flora.luau` (the Pilea, the trough's planting, the garden outside the window) and `Data/Wear.luau` (wear, condensation, silk, coin, fallen leaves) with Roblox's own features only: a material per surface (PBR through `MaterialService.Use2022Materials`, set in both Rojo projects), Future lighting (set in the Rojo projects), a soft warm sun picked to shine in through the window (`Habitat/SunPick`; the choice is in Lighting's attribute `SunPick` and Output), Atmosphere haze, Sky, clouds, bloom, colour grade and sun rays; a drawn window wall with glass and a view (sky, hills, houses, trees) while the physical back wall stays solid, climbable and invisible; dust motes and light shafts. The look is a worn, sunlit windowsill at the spider's scale (ROBLOX-21): cream enamel with bare wood showing at chips and along the front lip, grime in the corners and dust bunnies; the three Pilea leaves drawn as glossy pancakes with veins, a darker rim, a lighter underside and petioles over their pieces (`Habitat/Plants`; the pieces turn invisible, still solid and climbable, only when the drawing was built); a terracotta trough with a damp stain, soil, pebbles, moss and seedlings; outside the window a weathered window box of nine black-eyed Susans, with an invisible Neon ultraviolet layer (`Workspace.Decor.UVLayer`, attribute `UVOpacity`, for the spider-eye view), and flowering shrubs (since ROBLOX-95 the window box is two halves on the stone outer sill either side of the raised sash, and the shrubs stand on stems on a lawn at the garden's level); dark wood and a silk retreat in the crevice; a copper coin, fallen leaves, paint chips, a crack and crumbs; condensation and smudges on the glass (`Habitat/Patina`). All decor is non-colliding, untouchable and invisible to rays. The macro depth of field is on in the full tier unless the Workspace attribute `DepthOfField` is false. The looks are best effort: a lighting or decor failure is warned about in Output and the old look stays (the solid back wall stays visible if the drawn wall fails).

The flies are house flies (ROBLOX-22), 0.7 cm long, drawn by `src/Client/FlyBodies.luau` from the numbers in `src/Shared/Data/FlyLook.luau`: ellipsoids and rods, 39 parts a fly (four flies, 156 parts), the same at both detail tiers. A head with two big glossy red-brown compound eyes and a pale silver face stripe, a grey thorax with four dark stripes (thin slices that follow its curve), a grey-brown abdomen with a darker stripe down its back, a short dark proboscis, eight tiny bristles, six two-segment legs standing on the surface, and two thin glass wings (transparency 0.55, a dark vein along the front edge, a faint iridescent film after Shevtsova et al. 2011, PNAS 108:668-673) folded back in a shallow V. In the air each wing is a soft translucent blur that trembles a little (a beating wing would only strobe at 60 frames a second), the legs tuck in, the fly keeps its back to the sky (the normal it carries is that of the surface it last stood on) and the soft dark oval that stands in for the shadow on the surface (the parts cast none) goes. Every part is anchored, non-colliding, untouchable, invisible to rays and casts no shadow; a fly's position, heading and normal still come from the prey simulation (the prey profile stays species-neutral: this is only how the prototype is drawn) and nothing here feeds back. `tests/flies.luau` pins it.

Detail tiers: touch-only devices (phones, tablets) get the low tier, anything else the full tier; the Workspace attribute `Detail` set to "low" or "full" overrides the guess. Full is 693 parts for the first spider (492 hairs, 8 shells) and 1,156 decor parts (budget 1,500); low is 265 spider parts (134 hairs and 2 shells, no standing guard hairs, no idle motion) and 292 decor parts (budget 450); the other four spiders are 119 / 80 (peacock), 79 / 69 (ant mimic), 146 / 84 (Mexican redknee) and 148 / 84 (Greenbottle blue) at full / low, without bloom, sun rays, clouds, shafts, depth of field, the small details, the wear, the glass condensation, the leaves' veins and the finer planting. Frame time at either tier has not been measured in Studio or on a device (item 2 of the detail handoff's checklist).

The looks are `src/Shared/Data/Appearances.luau`, separate from species and physics. The first spider keeps the selected black-and-white pattern at every stage; the peacock spider, the ant mimic and the two tarantulas are costumes on the same body and the same rules. The rig uses anchored noncolliding parts and moves them with BulkMoveTo (a second call a frame only when a hair moved, ROBLOX-93). Growth, hunger and softness, twitch and the poses are in (LIFE, ROBLOX-35 on).
