# The Windowsill — active Roblox project

Open **TheWindowsill.rbxlx** in Roblox Studio and press **Play**. After updating Git, close/reopen the place so Studio reads the new file. Keep this folder name: `.gdignore` keeps the frozen Godot project from importing it. Scale remains 1 Godot WU = 1 stud; gravity is 35.

## Controls: one scheme, as in the Godot game

Every action of the frozen Godot game (`scripts/core/input_setup.gd` and its README) against the port. Keyboard and mouse, a controller and touch work together: an action is held while any of them holds it (ROBLOX-08). The words on screen name the device you used last. H shows or hides the controls card.

## The look: a cute, fuzzy spider and a lit windowsill (ROBLOX-13, ROBLOX-14)

The spider keeps the daughter's design exactly (tests/look.luau pins it) and is cuter and fuzzier: a bigger, rounder head, big glossy eyes with catchlights, fluffy palps, a bold jumper's tufts behind the eyes (no eyebrows, mouth or smile: docs/SPEC.md), a short coat of thin flat locks combed toward the rear (black on the black areas, white only on the white spots and the band), a few guard hairs, a short side fringe and four faint dark fur shells that soften the outline, sized for the 5-stud camera, hairy legs, and a little idle motion (breathing, a slow head tilt, palp drumming) when it stands still. The idle motion is drawn-rig only; feet, root part and movement never see it. Every number is a knob in `src/Shared/Data/Appearances.luau`.

The world is drawn from `src/Shared/Data/Graphics.luau` with Roblox's own features only: a material per surface (PBR through `MaterialService.Use2022Materials`, set in both Rojo projects), Future lighting (set in the Rojo projects), a soft warm sun picked to shine in through the window (`Habitat/SunPick`; the choice is in Lighting's attribute `SunPick` and Output), Atmosphere haze, Sky, clouds, bloom, colour grade and sun rays; a drawn window wall with glass and a view (sky, hills, houses, trees) while the physical back wall stays solid, climbable and invisible; dust motes, light shafts, paint chips, a crack and a crumb. All decor is non-colliding, untouchable and invisible to rays. Depth of field is off unless the Workspace attribute `DepthOfField` is true. The looks are best effort: a lighting or decor failure is warned about in Output and the old look stays (the solid back wall stays visible if the drawn wall fails).

Detail tiers: touch-only devices (phones, tablets) get the low tier, anything else the full tier; the Workspace attribute `Detail` set to "low" or "full" overrides the guess. Full is 624 spider parts (475 hairs) and 76 decor parts; low is 266 spider parts (145 hairs, no standing guard hairs, one fur shell, no idle motion) and 34 decor parts, without bloom, sun rays, clouds, shafts or the small details. Nothing has been seen in Roblox Studio or timed on a device.

The first appearance is `src/Shared/Data/Appearances.luau`, separate from species and physics. It keeps the selected black-and-white pattern at every recorded stage. The rig uses anchored noncolliding parts and one BulkMoveTo per frame. Growth, hunger/softness and full pose/twitch parity still need the LIFE port.
